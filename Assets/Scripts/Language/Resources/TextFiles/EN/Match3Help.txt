Sifter Minigame: How to Play
#
In this minigame, you'll identify bones by matching together 3 of a kind or more. Each level will have you match on different condition-some will have you match the same bone together, while others will have you match bones that come from the same species. Return to this menu to check the current requirement!
#
Controls:
Left click and Drag: move bones
Right click: show bone
Scroll: zoom in on bone
Right click and drag: rotate bone
