FR:There is a main quest - to tell a good story about this fossil bone - and there are other smaller quests to complete along the way.
#
To figure out which quest you are working on or to see how far you have gotten, you may open up the quest menu.

You can pin quests and check progress. You can see all the facts you've collected towards the main quest as well.
#
To end the game, you may choose to go to the birthday party and tell your story at any time. 

But if you want that story to be a good one, you should solve some mini quests first!
