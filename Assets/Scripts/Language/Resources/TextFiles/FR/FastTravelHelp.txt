FR:You have several methods of fast travel once you have completed the tutorial quest.
#
You may use the map to travel in the present, and anywhere you have already visited in the past. This is especially helpful if you, say, get stuck in a lake. Or fall off the map. Available waypoints will be light blue.

Use the 'Switch Map View' button in the upper left corner to view the Past or Modern maps. 
#
Your Astrolabe is your time travel device, but it only works when you have something new to visit in the past. It will show you that there is something new by displaying a star on the menu item.
